## Mission-specific regression priorities

The following are new-engine acceptance designs, not invented original coordinates, timings, counts or scripts. Obtain actual predicates from the source program and reference runs before assigning expected values.

- **Grouped objectives:** identify the real actor/program bindings first; test the boundary before, at and after its transition, and verify that the wrong actor, wrong session or repeated event cannot satisfy it.
- **World damage state:** identify the real actor/program bindings first; test the boundary before, at and after its transition, and verify that the wrong actor, wrong session or repeated event cannot satisfy it.
- **Completion ordering:** identify the real actor/program bindings first; test the boundary before, at and after its transition, and verify that the wrong actor, wrong session or repeated event cannot satisfy it.

Test combinations of these transitions, not only each in isolation. A branch involving two systems is not certified when both individual systems compile. Save the relevant pre-transition and post-transition normalized state to the private evidence directory and compare actor identity, ownership, objective state and pending events.

## Required acceptance matrix

**M03-BIND:** Find the corresponding original catalog/program/world identities. Confirm the title against local strings; do not key runtime logic by this discovery label.

**M03-SUCCESS:** Record an ordinary-play route that reaches the authored success state. List the actual objective/event sequence after observing it; do not inject success or delete enemies externally.

**M03-FAILURE:** Exercise every discovered critical failure cause and record the visible result, retry path and absence of rewards.

**M03-ORDER:** Identify independent objectives and test at least one permitted alternative order. If the program forbids reordering, prove the gate prevents premature completion.

**M03-RETRY:** Restart from a mid-progress failure and verify all actors, timers, equipment, inputs, sounds and UI return to the authored initial state.

**M03-CONTINUITY:** Advance to the next campaign step, save/exit/reopen and verify progression, records, roster and media links. For the final mission verify ending and final profile state.

**M03-PRESENTATION:** Review original world/aircraft/cockpit, dialogue, music, effects, map and cinematic transitions. Headless traces cannot pass this row.

**M03-DIFFICULTY:** Discover all applicable difficulty variants and cover their changed branches/parameters. Do not assume a fixed count or identical spawning.

**M03-OPTIONAL:** Enumerate actual optional/stunt/reward branches from data and reference observation; verify each eligible result without granting every reward automatically.

## Data binding checklist

Resolve the canonical mission id; installation/rules hash; title string; program and source map; world group/variant; initial player and wingmate configurations; actor/spawn/route sets; objective graph; interaction authorizations; script/native coverage; required geometry/collision/materials; audio/dialogue/video/camera cues; initial and terminal campaign state; optional/stunt/reward ids; difficulty branches; exact failure/success precedence. Unknown values remain null and block readiness.

Do not manufacture numeric values from a walkthrough. Spawn counts, distance tolerances, timings, durability, rewards and object ids come from actual source records or a separately documented measurement. The public research did not recover these values.
