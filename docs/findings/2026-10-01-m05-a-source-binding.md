## What changed

Production (`crates/cs_content/src/campaign_bindings.rs`):

- `TitleForm` (`Verbatim` | `RegionPrefixedLongName`) and the pure
  `title_form(display, title)` decide which of two observed display forms of a
  localized row carries a title.
- `TitleConfirmation` (`Confirmed { row_id, form }` | `Uncarried` |
  `Ambiguous`) and `SourceContext::confirm_title`, which `bind` now calls
  instead of comparing rows inline.
- `SourceContext::other_spellings(position, confirmed)`, and a new `unknowns`
  entry the binding records when the confirmation came through the long-name
  form and the installation spells the same campaign position differently
  elsewhere.
- `UNCARRIED_TITLE_REFUSAL` and `AMBIGUOUS_TITLE_REFUSAL`, so an unconfirmed
  title's reason names its own cause instead of a single shared sentence.

Tests and records: `crates/cs_app/tests/campaign/m05_a.rs` (eight

## Why a production change was needed

`missions/README.md` declares M05's discovery title as `The Union Jack's
Revenge`. The installation carries that string **in no row verbatim**. What it
carries, read from `GOSDATA/ASSETS/BINARIES/langui.dll`, is:

| Row | Text | Form |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 3454 | `[AB14I]Hawaii - The Union Jack's Revenge` | region-prefixed long name, **tail equals the declared title exactly** |
| 3484 | `[AB14I]Union Jack's Revenge` | bare short name of the same campaign position, **no leading article** |

Under M03-A's rule — one row whose display text equals the discovery title —
M05's title, mission, world and program identities all stayed unresolved, so
the stage's minimum scenario could not be met by running M03-A's machinery a
fourth time. The same condition holds for M09, M11, M14, M15, M20, M22 and M23
(see "What is still unknown" below).

`missions/M05.md` says the discovery cue is a research label, not a verified
catalog id, that title spelling and localization must be checked against the
owner's original installation, and that the original localized string wins.
That is the rule this change implements, in the narrowest form the evidence
supports: the original *does* carry the declared title, byte for byte, in the
long-name row's tail. Nothing fuzzy happens; the tail must equal the title
exactly, and the region prefix is dropped without ever being compared with
anything or interpreted as a region.

The precedence is deliberate and conservative: **a verbatim row always wins.**
Six declared titles sit in both display forms at the same campaign offset
(M12, M16, M18, M19, M21, M24), and preferring the verbatim row for those is
only defensible because it does not choose between two different identities —
`accept_m05_a_the_verbatim_form_wins_where_both_display_forms_carry_the_title`
measures that both forms agree on the campaign position before accepting the
preference. M05 is currently the only work order that needs the long-name form
(`long_only == 1`); the test asserts that count so a future installation or
inventory change cannot make it pass vacuously.

## What is different at M05

- **The world group is shared by three campaign missions.** `world/c1` is M02's
  (`ch1/m02`), M04's (`ch1/m04`) *and* M05's (`ch1/m05`), so — unlike M03's
  single-mission `world/c1b` — the world identity does not identify this
  mission. What does is the program archive inside the shared group: three
  distinct paths with three distinct SHA-256 digests, pinned by
  `accept_m05_a_the_world_group_is_shared_and_the_program_singles_the_mission_out`.
- **The same directory again holds non-campaign subdirectories.** `ZBD/C1`
  carries `IA1`, `MP1`, `MP2` and `MP3` beside `M02`, `M04` and `M05`, each with
  its own `zrdr.zbd`. Their role is **not established** and is not claimed; no
  span of `M05.json` points into them, and the test asserts that.
- **The mission's own name is spelled two ways by the original program.** This
  is the finding, not a defect: the binding reports it in `unknowns` and stays
  `verified: false`.

## What is not claimed

`verified` is `false`; `closure_sha256` is null. `unknowns` in `M05.json` lists
every checklist entry this stage does not bind (actors, objectives,
interactions, media, rewards, difficulty branches, failure/success precedence,
progression) **plus** the title-spelling difference, which is deliberately not
reconciled — the record does not claim which of the two original spellings is
"the" name of the mission. Only the `mission_identity` cell of M05's campaign
record is `Complete`; coverage is not ready. The three M05 regression
priorities (wave lifecycle, protected-actor damage, timed completion) have no
actor, objective or predicate bound and remain for M05-B once the mission
program is decoded. The chain from title to mission directory is still an
inference (`ClaimStatus::Inferred`), not an observation, and nothing here is
`verified_original`.

## Review notes (bunny-2/bunny-2 as reviewing agent, 2026-10-01)

The retail facts in this finding were re-derived independently, not taken from
the implementer's tables: `cs-inspect config --file langui.dll` decoded all
1616 localizable strings, and the display texts of the two campaign-length
blocks were read from that report directly. Confirmed: rows 3450–3473 are the
region-prefixed long names grouping `5/5/5/5/4` (Hawaii, Northwest, Hollywood,
Rocky Mountains, Manhattan) which equals the directory layout's chapter sizes;
3480–3503 are the bare short names; the declared M05 title is carried by
exactly one row, as the tail of 3454; six declared titles sit in both forms at
the same block offset; seven sit in neither. `ZBD/C1/M05/zrdr.zbd` is 115570
bytes with SHA-256 `0ae0341c…f8c34fe`.

Judgement accepted, with one documentation correction: confirming a title
against the exact tail of a region-prefixed long name is a byte-for-byte
comparison against a string the original program really carries, the join it
feeds is the join M02-A already established and M02-A's own guard still
refuses on a contradiction, the resulting identity stays `ClaimStatus::Inferred`,
and the second spelling is reported rather than chosen between. The
verbatim-wins precedence is justified by the measured agreement of the six
both-form titles, which the acceptance suite asserts before the preference is
relied on.

Fixed in review:

- `TitleConfirmation::Ambiguous`'s doc comment claimed that "one verbatim row
  plus a long name naming the same title" is ambiguous. It is not: the
  implementation — and `…the_verbatim_form_wins_where_both_display_forms_carry_the_title`
  — confirm verbatim in that case. A public enum whose documentation denies
  the behaviour it has is a real defect; the comment now matches the code.
- `SourceContext::confirm_title`'s closing clause ("never a row that carries
  the title in a second form as well") was ambiguous about exactly that
  precedence. It now states the rule plainly and says plainly that the choice
  between two forms naming different missions is a measurement the acceptance
  stage makes, not something this function can see.
- `crates/cs_app/tests/campaign/evidence.rs`: the rebase that brought M04-A in
  left M02-A's doc comment attached to `RETAIL_TESTS_M05_A` and
  `SYNTHETIC_TESTS_M02_A` with none. The M05-A constants are moved after
  M02-A's pair and every constant carries its own comment.
- The failure arms of `…the_title_is_confirmed_only_through_the_long_name_form`
  asserted only that the recorded refusal contains *one of* the two refusal
  sentences, so swapping `UNCARRIED_TITLE_REFUSAL` and `AMBIGUOUS_TITLE_REFUSAL`
  passed. Each arm is now paired with the `TitleConfirmation` it must produce

  `…the_title_is_confirmed_only_through_the_long_name_form`; on the code as

- "What is still unknown" claimed the seven uncarried titles differ from the
