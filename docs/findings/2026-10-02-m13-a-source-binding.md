## What is different at M13

- **The middle mission of chapter 3.** Position 12 is the third of chapter 3's
  five missions (positions 10..15), so unlike M12 neither the layout's chapter
  boundary nor the long names' region boundary falls on it or beside it.
  `accept_m13_a_the_position_is_interior_to_the_third_chapter_and_its_region_group`
  asserts the layout's chapter group and the long names' third region group
  span the same positions, that the position is at least two rows from either
  end, and that the neighbouring positions are in the same chapter.
- **The two localized rows differ only by the region prefix.** The short-name
  row equals the declared title verbatim; the second row at the same index
  carries a region prefix (` - ` separator) and is a different string, so the
  confirmation is a fact about one localized string, as at M12.
- **The world group is the whole chapter.** `world/c3` holds all five
  chapter-3 missions, so the world row identifies the chapter, not M13; unlike
  chapter 2 there is no separate `c3b` directory. The binding cites only its
  own program archive.
