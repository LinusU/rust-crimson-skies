## What is different at M07

- **It is chapter 2's second mission, one row after the boundary M06-A bound.**
  M06 is campaign position 5, the first mission of chapter 2. M07 is position
  6, chapter 2 mission 2. Both the directory layout's chapter boundary and the
  localized long names' region boundary fall on the chapter's *first* row
  (position 5); M07's position is that row advanced by its mission number.
  `accept_m07_a_the_position_is_the_second_mission_of_the_second_chapter`
  asserts both boundaries and that the previous position is the chapter's
  mission 1, so a join that swapped chapter 2's first two missions — both in
  `world/c2`, so the world row cannot separate them — is caught.
- **The world group is shared with M06's own mission.** M07 and M06 resolve the
  *same* world id, `world/c2`, but different mission and program ids. The C2
  group holds four campaign missions (positions 5, 6, 7, 9); chapter 2's fifth
  mission, M04 (position 8), lives in the separate `c2b` directory.
  `accept_m07_a_neither_the_world_group_nor_the_mission_number_identifies_the_mission`
  pins that the world id names no single mission, and that mission number `2`
  names one mission in every chapter, so neither key identifies M07. Only the
  campaign position does.
- **The original spellings disagree, as at M03 and M06.** The short-name row
  and the region-prefixed long name are different strings;
  `accept_m07_a_the_original_name_is_confirmed_against_the_local_strings`
  pins what is actually confirmed.

## What is not claimed

does not bind (actors, objectives, interactions, media, rewards, difficulty
branches, failure/success precedence, progression, `closure_sha256`) is listed
in `unknowns` of `M07.json`. Only the `mission_identity` cell of M07's campaign
record is `Complete`; coverage is not ready. The three M07 regression
priorities (moving pickup, forced plane swap, persistent input ownership) have
no actor, objective or predicate bound; they remain for M07-B once the mission
program is decoded. The chain from title to mission directory is still an
inference (`ClaimStatus::Inferred`), not an observation, and nothing here is
`verified_original`.
