## What is different at M04

- **Neither the world group nor the mission number is a mission identity.**
  `world/c1` holds three campaign missions (M02, M04, M05) and the number `4`
  names one mission in *every* chapter (`ch1-m04` … `ch5-m04`), so two
  identities a consumer might key on both coincide at M04 and neither singles
  the mission out. Only the campaign position selects the one mission the
  record binds;
  `accept_m04_a_neither_the_world_group_nor_the_mission_number_identifies_the_mission`
  pins both axes and that the record binds exactly the `ch1` mission its
  position selects, never a world-group sibling or a same-number mission in
  another chapter.
- **A fourth position on the same join.** The join and its corroboration are
  M02-A's; this stage exercises them at position 3, so a join that happened to
  be right for positions 0, 1 and 2 cannot pass. The short-name row block M04's
  title resolves through carries no region prefix and cannot corroborate
  itself; the corroboration comes from the region-prefixed long-name rows on
  the same installation.
