## What is different at M10

- **The position ends a chapter and a region group.** M06-A pinned chapter
  2's leading edge; M10 is position 9, its *trailing* edge — the last mission
  of chapter 2 (`mission/ch2-m05`) and the last row of the localized long
  names' second region group. Both structures place their boundary
  immediately after position 9, so a one-row-off grouping at the *end* of a
  group — a mistake M06's leading-edge test cannot see — is caught here.
  `accept_m10_a_the_position_is_the_last_row_of_the_second_chapter_and_region_group`
  asserts both boundaries fall immediately after campaign position 9, using
  only group sizes, never a region name.
- **Campaign order is not directory order inside the chapter.** Chapter 2's
  missions are stored in two directories: `ZBD/C2` holds `M01`, `M02`, `M03`
  and `M05`, while `ZBD/C2B` holds `M04`. The campaign order — sorted by
  `(chapter, mission number)` — therefore crosses from `C2` into `C2B` at
  position 8 and *back* into `C2` at position 9, even though `C2B` sorts
  after `C2`. A join that ordered the chapter's missions by directory name
  would invert positions 8 and 9 and bind `ch2-m04` where `ch2-m05` belongs.
  `accept_m10_a_the_chapter_order_is_not_the_directory_order` pins the
  inversion: the previous position shares M10's chapter but not its world
  group, and `c2b` sorts after `c2`.
- **The world group is shared, and M10 shares it with M06.** `world/c2`
  holds four of chapter 2's five campaign missions (positions 5, 6, 7, 9), so
  the world row alone cannot identify M10. The same test asserts the bound
  world id equals M06's — the shared directory is the fact being measured —
  while the campaign position differs, and that the record cites only its own
  program archive, never a sibling's.
- **The original spellings disagree, as at M03 and M06.** The short-name row
  and the region-prefixed long name are different strings;
  `accept_m10_a_the_original_name_is_confirmed_against_the_local_strings`
  pins what is actually confirmed.

## What is not claimed

`verified` is `false`, `closure_sha256` null, and every checklist entry M10-A
does not bind (actors, objectives, interactions, media, rewards, difficulty
branches, failure/success precedence, progression, `closure_sha256`) is listed
in `unknowns` of `M10.json`. Only the `mission_identity` cell of M10's campaign
record is `Complete`; coverage is not ready. The three M10 regression
priorities (protected neutral actor, attached payloads, air and surface
threats) have no actor, objective or predicate bound; they remain for M10-B
once the mission program is decoded. The chain from title to mission directory
is still an inference (`ClaimStatus::Inferred`), not an observation, and
nothing here is `verified_original`.
