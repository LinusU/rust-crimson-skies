## What is different at M18

- **The position is interior, not a boundary.** Position 17 is the *third* of
  chapter 4's five missions (15..19), so — as at M12 and M13, and unlike M16 —
  neither the layout's chapter boundary (row 15) nor the localized long names'
  fourth region boundary falls on the row or next to it; both are two rows
  above. `accept_m18_a_the_position_is_interior_to_the_fourth_chapter_and_its_region_group`
  asserts that both structures place the group's first and one-past-last rows
  at campaign positions 15 and 20. Chapter 4 is also the *last full* chapter
  (the layout's sizes are `[5, 5, 5, 5, 4]`), so the boundary the test compares
  is a real one: a long-name table that regrouped its last group as full would
  contradict the layout and stop the join entirely.
- **Neither the world group nor the mission number identifies M18.** `world/c4`
  holds all five chapter-4 missions, and the mission number `3` names a mission
  directory in *every* chapter: `ZBD/C1B/M03`, `ZBD/C2/M03`, `ZBD/C3/M03`,
  `ZBD/C4/M03` and `ZBD/C5/M03`. Their program archives are five different
  files with five different digests (measured through
  `cs_content::campaign_bindings::campaign_layout`), so no byte-level tie
  collapses the reuse. Exactly one mission sits at the intersection of M18's
  world group and its mission number, and the record cites only its own
  archive.
- **The two display forms agree about the title.** The short name at row 3497
  is `Deceit at Devil's Horn` and the region-prefixed long name at row 3467
  ends with that same text, so — unlike M05-A, where the short name read
  `Union Jack's Revenge` and the declared title read `The Union Jack's
  Revenge` — the record carries no title-spelling note, and the suite asserts
  it does not invent one. The confirmed form is `Verbatim`, from the second
  campaign-length row block (3480..3503); the corroborating region-prefixed
  block is the first (3450..3473), so the corroboration does not come from
  M18's own block.
- **The title's cited span is the row's own bytes, not its block.** The
  `RT_STRING` block that encloses row 3497 (`95304 + 876`) holds the short
  names of sixteen other missions, so a span of it does not identify a
  string. M18-A originally cited that whole block, as every binding did
  before M16-A-FU1 (#478); the review's rebase brought in that fix, the
  record was re-pinned to `95792 + 60`, and
  `…_source_derived_binding_has_no_unresolved_critical_dependencies` now pins
  the property rather than only the record: the cited bytes must carry M18's
  confirmed row **and no other row of either campaign-length block**.
  Containment alone would not have proved it — the block carries this row's
  text too — so the check runs both ways.
- **A confirmed row can still select no position — and M18's own region prefix
  is such a row.** This is the refusal arm no earlier binding stage exercised
  on real data. The installation carries `Rocky Mountains` as a standalone
  localized row (id 1223), so `SourceContext::confirm_title` *confirms* it and
  `SourceContext::bind` then resolves no campaign position at all: the row sits
  in a run of 38 rows, not in any block exactly as long as the 24-mission
  campaign. The resulting record leaves exactly `mission_id`,
  `program_source_map` and `world_group_variant` unresolved, each refusing
  with `SHORT_ROW_BLOCK_REFUSAL`, while `title_string` and `installation_hash`
  are resolved — confirmation and position are separate rules.
  `accept_m18_a_m18s_own_region_prefix_is_a_confirmed_row_that_selects_no_position`
  measures this on the installation, reading the prefix out of M18's own second
  spelling rather than a constant;
  `accept_m18_a_a_confirmed_row_outside_every_campaign_block_selects_no_position`
  proves the same production predicate on authored values so CI runs it.

## What is not claimed

`verified` is `false`, `closure_sha256` null, and every checklist entry M18-A
does not bind (actors, objectives, interactions, media, rewards, difficulty
branches, failure/success precedence, progression, `closure_sha256`) is listed
in `unknowns` of `M18.json`. Only the `mission_identity` cell of M18's campaign
record is `Complete`; coverage is not ready. The three M18 regression
priorities (alternative action order, release dependencies, rescue
interaction) have no actor, objective or predicate bound; they remain for
M18-B once the mission program is decoded. The title-to-directory join is
still an inference (`ClaimStatus::Inferred`) and nothing here is
`verified_original`.

The `hostage exchange` discovery cue is a research label from the public
guide. Nothing measured at this stage establishes it, and nothing in the
record or the runtime is keyed by it.

The note in `missions/M18.md` that "M18 in the English guide contains a
spelling discrepancy" is **not** reproduced in this installation's English
rows: row 3497 (`[AB14I]Deceit at Devil's Horn`) and row 3467
(`[AB14I]Rocky Mountains - Deceit at Devil's Horn`) both carry the declared
title's spelling exactly, and no other row in the table contains the words
`deceit` or `devil` in any spelling. The sheet's warning may refer to the
guide's own prose or to a different localization; this stage can only say that
the discrepancy is not observable in the installation's English string table.
Which mission the guide's label names is therefore established by the
inventory's title matching the retail short-name row, not by the cue.

## Review (bunny-alpha-1, fresh context)

The reviewer re-derived M18's retail facts from `$CS_GAME_DIR` through
production code without reading the binding's own output, and every claim in
the table above held: the title is carried by exactly one row, 3497; the
second display form is 3467, `Rocky Mountains - Deceit at Devil's Horn`; the
region prefix is a standalone row, 1223; the two campaign-length row blocks
are `3450..3473` (the region-prefixed one) and `3480..3503` (the short names,
which is where 3497 sits); the chapter sizes are `[5, 5, 5, 5, 4]`; and
`ZBD/C4/M03/zrdr.zbd` hashes to `0eff1e94…`, the digest the record cites.

The reviewer also repeated the mutation work independently and **found one
more surviving mutation**: dropping only the `unknowns.is_empty()` condition
from `SourceBinding::is_verified` left all twelve tests green, because M18's
own record is unverified for four separate reasons at once — its unknowns, its
null `closure_sha256` and its empty `evidence_ids` — so any one condition
dropping was masked by the other two. `SourceBinding::validate` does not close
it either: it compares `is_verified()` against
`unresolved_critical().is_empty() && unknowns.is_empty()`, which a record with
an empty `closure_sha256` and no evidence claims satisfies either way. The
implementer's `is_verified` mutation was the stronger one (reduce it to the
critical dependencies alone), which *is* caught, so the reported gap was
narrower than the real one. The reviewer closed it with
`accept_m18_a_a_verified_needs_every_condition_and_not_only_the_dependencies`,
which starts from a record satisfying all four conditions and drops each one
in turn; re-checked, it now catches the mutation that survived.
