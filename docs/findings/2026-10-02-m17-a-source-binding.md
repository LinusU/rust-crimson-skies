## What is different at M17

- **The declared title is carried by the bare short name and by no long
  name.** Row 3496 reads `[AB14I]The Pirate's Duel`. The region-prefixed long
  name for the *same* campaign position, row 3466, reads
  `[AB14I]Rocky Mountains - Nathan Zachary & The Pirate's Duel`: its tail
  after the observed `" - "` separator is `Nathan Zachary & The Pirate's
  Duel`, which is **not** the declared title. So `SourceContext::confirm_title`
  confirms M17 `TitleForm::Verbatim` and `TitleForm::RegionPrefixedLongName`
  matches nothing at all for this title.

  The consequence is the opposite of M05-A and M16-A: because the
  confirmation did not come through the long-name form, `SourceContext::bind`
  records **no** `title spelling` unknown, and `M17.json` carries only the
  thirteen checklist entries every source binding leaves unknown.
  `accept_m17_a_only_the_short_name_row_carries_the_declared_title` asserts
  that absence positively, so a stage that copied M05-A's or M16-A's
  expectation cannot pass here.

  Both spellings still agree about *which* mission: binding from the long
  name's own tail resolves to the same campaign position and therefore the
  same `mission/ch4-m02` / `world/c4` / `script/c4-m02-zrdr`, while recording
  the spelling difference that M17's own record does not reach. That is what
  lets `M17-BIND` say "the original localized string wins" without keying
  runtime logic to the discovery label. Neither claim says the *name* is
  settled; the guide's label and the installation's two spellings remain three
  different strings.

- **The position is interior, one row past a boundary.** Position 16 is the
  *second* mission of chapter 4 (positions 15..19). The layout's chapter
  boundary and the localized long names' fourth region group both fall on row
  15, one row above M17, and the region prefix really does change there
  (`Hollywood` at row 3464, `Rocky Mountains` from row 3465). A join that
  divided either group one row off would move M17's bound identity — this
  stage confirms the chapter-4 boundary from the *far* side, where M16-A
  measured it from the near side.

- **Neither the world group nor the mission number names this mission.** The
  world group `world/c4` holds all five chapter-4 missions, and the mission
  number `2` is used by one mission in *every* chapter (`ZBD/C1/M02`,
  `ZBD/C2/M02`, `ZBD/C3/M02`, `ZBD/C4/M02`, `ZBD/C5/M02`). Only the campaign
  position separates them, and behind it the program archive — which is
  byte-length-distinct across chapter 4 (`56493`, `31647`, `90978`, `61137`,
  `42642`), so M17's `zrdr.zbd` is the chapter's shortest and a citation
  taken one row off would not validate against the record.

## Measured finding: seven declared titles are carried by no retail row

Binding **every** declared inventory title through production code shows that
seven of the 24 discovery labels match neither display form:

| Work order | Declared title | Short-name row | Long-name row (tail after `" - "`) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| M09 | `Perils for Blake` | `Peril for Blake` (3488) | `Peril for Paladin Blake` (3458) |
| M11 | `The Stolen Scarlet` | `The Stolen Starlet` (3490) | `Nathan Zachary & The Stolen Starlet` (3460) |
| M14 | `Clash of the Dreadnaughts` | `Clash of Dreadnaughts` (3493) | `Nathan Zachary & The Clash of Dreadnaughts` (3463) |
| M15 | `The Fight for the Figaro` | `Fight for the FIGAROA` (3494) | `The Fight for the FIGAROA` (3464) |
| M20 | `Unholy Alliance` | `The Unholy Alliance` (3499) | `Nathan Zachary & The Unholy Alliance` (3469) |
| M22 | `Runaway Witness` | `The Runaway Witness` (3501) | `Nathan Zachary & The Runaway Witness` (3471) |
| M23 | `Criminal Exodus` | `The Criminal Exodus` (3502) | `Nathan Zachary & The Criminal Exodus` (3472) |

Each resolves `TitleConfirmation::Uncarried`, so `MissionId`, `TitleString`,
`ProgramSourceMap` and `WorldGroupVariant` all stay unresolved and the record
yields no catalog identity at all. The differences are of four kinds: a
plural/singular change (`Perils`/`Peril`), a different colour word
(`Scarlet`/`Starlet`), a dropped or added article (`Unholy Alliance`/`The
Unholy Alliance`), and a proper-noun spelling (`Figaro`/`FIGAROA`).

This is a *discovery-label* spelling problem, not a missing-data problem: the
installation carries each of these missions twice, and only the guide's
spelling fails to match. It is already noted in `missions/bindings/README.md`
and `docs/findings/2026-10-01-m05-a-source-binding.md`; this stage re-measured
it over all 24 titles and confirms the set is exactly those seven. It is
**not** changed here: `campaign-inventory.tsv` declares the guide's titles,
`SourceContext::confirm_title` compares exactly by design (no fuzzy matching),
and deciding which retail mission a differently-spelled label names is an
owner question, not one an agent should settle by editing the denominator. It
does not affect M17, whose title is carried verbatim.

Note also that the M17 sheet's line *"M18 in the English guide contains a
spelling discrepancy; the original localized string wins"* is **not**
reproducible for M18 from this installation: M18's declared `Deceit at Devil's
Horn` is carried verbatim by row 3497, and its long name (row 3467) reads the
same string with the `Rocky Mountains` prefix. Recorded as a measurement, not
acted on: `missions/M17.md` and `missions/M18.md` are protected and M18-A is
a separate task.
