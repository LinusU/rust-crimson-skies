## What is different at M06

- **The position crosses into a second chapter.** M01-A, M02-A and M03-A bound
  positions 0, 1 and 2 — every one of them a chapter-1 mission. M06 is position
  5, the first mission of chapter 2 (`mission/ch2-m01`). Its world group changes
  from the `c1*` directories to `c2`, so the join is exercised at a position
  after the first chapter.
- **The chapter boundary and the region-group boundary coincide.** The
  localized long names group into `[5,5,5,5,4]`, the same shape as
  `chapter_sizes()`. Position 5 is the first row of the *second* group
  (`Hawaii`×5 then `Northwest`×5): a join that divided the long-name rows one
  row off (4/6 or 6/4) would contradict the layout and bind nothing.
  `accept_m06_a_the_position_is_the_first_row_of_the_second_chapter_and_region_group`
  asserts both boundaries fall on campaign position 5, using only group sizes,
  never a region name.
- **The world group is neither the mission nor the chapter.** `world/c2` holds
  four campaign missions (M01, M02, M03, M05; positions 5, 6, 7, 9), so the
  world row alone cannot identify M06. Chapter 2's fifth mission, M04 (position
  8), lives in the separate `c2b` directory, so the chapter is not one world
  group either.
  `accept_m06_a_the_world_group_holds_several_missions_and_not_the_whole_chapter`
  pins both halves and that the binding cites only its own program archive, not
  a sibling's. The role of `C2B` (and of `IA1`/`MP1`/`MP3`) is **not
  established**; nothing read here says what those directories are.
