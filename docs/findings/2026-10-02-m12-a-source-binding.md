- **First binding in chapter 3.** Position 11 is the second mission of
  chapter 3 (positions 10..15). `accept_m12_a_the_position_is_inside_the_third_chapter_and_its_region_group`
  asserts the layout's chapter group and the long names' third region group
  both span exactly those positions, and that the previous and next positions
  are in the same chapter.
- **The two localized spellings agree.** Unlike M08 (`Plot` / `Pit`), the
  short-name row and the region-prefixed long name carry the same title; the
  declared title matches the short-name row verbatim, so no second-form rule
  is needed.
- **The world group is the whole chapter.** `world/c3` holds all five
  chapter-3 missions, so the world row identifies the chapter, not M12; unlike
  chapter 2 there is no separate `c3b` directory. The binding cites only its
  own program archive.
